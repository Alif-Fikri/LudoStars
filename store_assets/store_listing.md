## App name (max 30 char)

## Short description (max 80 char)

## Full description (max 4000 char)

Ludo Stars brings the classic Ludo board game to your phone — simple, fun, and ready to play anytime, no internet connection required!

🎲 HOW TO PLAY
Race all 4 of your tokens from home to the finish, roll the dice, and be the first to bring every token home. Easy to learn, but still full of strategy and suspense on every turn.

🤖 PLAY VS COMPUTER
No friends around? Challenge the AI anytime. Choose your number of players and token color to match your style.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 LOCAL MULTIPLAYER
Gather your family or friends around one device and play pass-and-play multiplayer — just take turns passing the phone, perfect for game night together.

✨ FEATURES
- The classic Ludo game you know and love
- Play vs Computer (AI opponents)
- Local multiplayer (pass-and-play on one device)
- Simple controls, fun for all ages
- Free to play

Ludo Stars is free to play and supported by ads. No in-app purchases are required to enjoy the full game.

Download Ludo Stars now and enjoy this classic dice board game anytime, anywhere!
